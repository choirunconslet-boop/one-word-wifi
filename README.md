# One Word for Your Wi-Fi

Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Word for Your Wi-Fi"
(`u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72`).

`main.ts` writes a single word to `/output/entry.txt`: **Gaslighting**.

Full bars, nothing loads — the Wi-Fi insists everything is fine while
nothing works, making you doubt your own connection. Funny at once, true
of every home network, and it works as a network name.
